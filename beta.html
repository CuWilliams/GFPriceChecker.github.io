The Beta Has Closed

GF PriceChecker is no longer in development, and the public TestFlight beta is closed to new testers. Budget 2026 proposes a flat $1,000 claim for celiac disease, with no receipts to keep, which removes most of the record-keeping the app was built for.

Already Testing?

Export your records now and keep them somewhere safe: a backup from Settings, and your PDF or CSV records from the Reports tab. TestFlight builds expire, and once the last one does, the app will no longer open. Your data has only ever lived on your device, so an export is the only other copy.

Thank you for testing. Your feedback shaped every build.
