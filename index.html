Why GF PriceChecker Exists

For Canadians diagnosed with celiac disease, a gluten-free diet is a medical necessity — not a lifestyle choice. Gluten-free staples routinely cost 100–500% more than their conventional equivalents, creating a significant and ongoing financial burden for patients and their families.

A Medical Requirement

Canada's Medical Expense Tax Credit (METC) provides partial financial relief — but only for the price differential between gluten-free and regular products, and only with receipts to prove it.

A Complex Claim Process

Claiming the METC requires retaining every receipt, calculating the price differential for each product, and submitting detailed records at tax time — a process most Canadians with celiac disease find too complex to use effectively.

Celiac Canada Advocacy

Celiac Canada is actively lobbying the federal government to replace the METC with a simple, flat refundable tax credit of $1,000 per adult and $600 per child — eliminating the administrative burden entirely. Petition e-6853, supported by 33,000 Canadians, was presented in Parliament in February 2026.

GF PriceChecker fully supports this effort. We genuinely hope this app becomes redundant — and soon. Until it does, it's here to help.
